# 🔗 External Ecosystem & Portal Integrations
**Project:** EduBharat AI
**Scope:** Government Portals, Incubators, and Examination Systems

## 1. Overview
EduBharat AI is designed to integrate with state educational infrastructure and startup governance platforms to streamline administrative reporting and student verification.

## 2. Integration Roadmap

### 2.1 Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board (GSEB)
* **Target Interface:** Official GSEB Results & Student Data Gateway.
* **Functionality:** Direct sync of board seat numbers, Hall Ticket downloads, and semester examination result verification.
* **Protocol:** RESTful Webhooks and encrypted JSON payloads.

### 2.2 Student Startup and Innovation Policy (SSIP) & iHub Gujarat
* **Target Interface:** iHub Incubation & SSIP Grant Tracking Portals.
* **Functionality:** Exporting verified school usage metrics, active student counts, and teacher adoption data directly into grant evaluation reports.
* **Format:** Standardized CSV/PDF audit logs.

### 2.3 National Education Technology Infrastructure
* **DIKSHA / Vidya Samiksha Kendra (VSK):** Future synchronization with state attendance and learning outcome frameworks.
* **DigiLocker:** Direct storage integration for digital marksheet verification.
